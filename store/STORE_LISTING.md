## Full description (max 4000)
```
Tertiary HRMS is the official employee app for Tertiary Infotech Academy's Human Resource
Management System. Sign in with your work account to manage your HR essentials from anywhere.

FEATURES
• Dashboard — see your annual, medical and OT leave balances and expenses at a glance. Managers
  and HR also see the pending approvals queue.
• Leave — view balances and your full request history, and apply for leave in a few taps. The
  server computes working days and proration automatically.
• Payslips — browse your personal payslips and open the official PDF, rendered natively in-app.
• Expenses — track your expense claims and approved totals with clear status badges.
• Team — search the company directory.
• Calendar — public holidays, your events and approved leave, grouped by month.
• Timesheet — review the current week's hours and overtime.
• Profile — your full employee record.

SECURE BY DESIGN
The app is a secure client of your employer's HRMS — all data stays on the company server and is
accessed over encrypted HTTPS using your existing single sign-on session. Sign in with email and
password or a one-time email code.

For authorized Tertiary Infotech employees only. You need an existing HRMS account; contact your
HR department for access.
```
